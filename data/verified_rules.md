## 1. Funds Requirement

**Source:** https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/money

### Living Costs

| Location | Amount Per Month | Max Months |
|----------|------------------|------------|
| London | £1,529 | 9 months |
| Outside London | £1,171 | 9 months |

**London definition:** City of London + 32 London boroughs

### 28-Day Rule (CRITICAL)

**Exact wording from gov.uk:**
> "You must have this money for at least 28 days in a row. The end date 
> of the 28-day period must be within 31 days of the date you apply for 
> your visa."

**Example from gov.uk:**
> "If you submit your application on 1 January, you'd have to show that 
> the funds were in your bank account for at least the 28-day period 
> ending on 1 December."

### Course Fee

- Must pay tuition for 1 academic year (up to 9 months)
- Amount shown on CAS
- Exception: If in UK with valid visa for 12+ months

### Dependants

- Extra money required for each family member
- Amount: (verify separately)

### Loan / Sponsorship

- If using student loan: provide evidence from loan company
- If sponsored: provide evidence from sponsor
